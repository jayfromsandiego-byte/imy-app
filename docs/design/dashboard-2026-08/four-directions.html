One recommended act per visit · everything else recedes · four bottom tabs on phone

The Sharpened Desk

Our current desk, fixed by the research · status verdict · auto-advancing conveyor · care meter · quiet shelf

Evolution, not revolution. A one-line verdict answers "am I okay?" above the fold; the waiting queue becomes a conveyor (decide, and the next memory rises in place); secondary rooms shrink to a quiet shelf so only one thing is ever loud. The "nearly whole" meter is endowed progress, framed as care.
