The story

The obituary

This text shows on the page. Changes here update the page.

Eleanor Margaret Hayes was born in March of 1948, in the year the pier was rebuilt, and she never once lived more than a mile from the sea.

She kept a garden that fed half the street, wrote letters on paper because "a screen can't hold your hand," and kept a chair on the porch that was really for whoever needed it. Half Moon Bay knew her by the lemon cake she brought to every occasion, and to plenty of non-occasions besides.
